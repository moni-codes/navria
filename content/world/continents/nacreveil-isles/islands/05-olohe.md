# Olohe

The farthest and most isolated island of the Nacreveil Isles.

Olohe is associated with strange tides, disappearances, and deep-sea superstition.

## Known For

- deep-sea fishing
- strange tides
- ghost stories
- ancient sea caves

The surrounding waters are unusually deep, cold, and difficult to chart.

Compasses occasionally fail near certain coastal regions.

## Local Beliefs

The people of Olohe maintain many traditions considered strange by the rest of the Isles.

Some locals refuse to sail after sunset.

Others leave offerings near tide caves carved into the cliffs.

Many claim the sea around Olohe is inhabited by things older than the Isles themselves.

## Disappearances

People sometimes vanish from Olohe without explanation.

Most are blamed on storms or dangerous waters.

Not all disappearances leave evidence behind.

## Sea Caves

Ancient cave systems run beneath portions of the island coastline.

Some flood completely during high tide.

Others should not physically exist at all, according to Tidewarden surveyors.
