# Molokai

An industrial island known for coral harvesting, shipbuilding, and dangerous seasonal storms.

Molokai has become increasingly important following the discovery of kordite and the growing influence of mainland trade interests.

## Known For

- reef trimming
- coral shaping
- shipwrights
- underwater scaffold mining
- dangerous storms

Massive coral scaffolds stretch beneath portions of the coastline, allowing workers to harvest reef structures from beneath the sea itself.

The labor is dangerous, physically demanding, and increasingly profitable.

## Bluewater-Pierce Influence

Molokai is the primary stronghold of the Liege House **Bluewater-Pierce** within the Nacreveil Isles.

The family heavily invests in:
- industrial expansion
- maritime infrastructure
- kordite-powered technology
- mainland trade agreements

Many workers support this growing modernization effort, believing it offers prosperity and protection.

Others fear the island is losing its identity.

## Storm Seasons

Molokai’s surrounding waters are infamous for sudden electrical storms and violent reef currents.

Local sailors claim the storms have grown worse since the castle emerged.
