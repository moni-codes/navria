# Mother Isshara

## Overview

Mother Isshara is a renowned mystic believed to live somewhere within the Blackvine Jungle surrounding Greyford.

Stories concerning her vary wildly depending on who tells them.

Some describe her as:
- a healer
- a prophet
- a witch
- a spirit wearing mortal skin

## Reputation

Travelers occasionally seek her guidance when conventional wisdom fails them.

Many who claim to have met her report:
- impossible insight
- unsettling dreams afterward
- cryptic warnings
- strange acts of kindness

Others insist she does not exist at all.

## The Blackvine Jungle

The jungle surrounding Greyford is dense, humid, and notoriously difficult to navigate.

Locals claim the forest itself dislikes being mapped.
