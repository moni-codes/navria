# Lysoria Nacreveil

- **Title**: Tide Regent
- **Role**: Ruler of the Nacreveil Isles
- **Location**: [[world/nacreveil-isles/islands/kaivara|Iridesca]], Kaivara

## Overview

Lysoria Nacreveil rules the Nacreveil Isles from the coastal capital of Iridesca.

She commands the Tidewardens and oversees the increasingly unstable political climate surrounding the emergence of the drowned castle near Kaivara.

Though respected by many islanders, her authority is constantly pressured by:
- mainland influence
- competing Liege Houses
- unrest between the islands
- growing fear surrounding recent supernatural events

## Reputation

Lysoria is regarded as calm, politically calculating, and deeply protective of the Isles.

Her critics claim she hesitates too long before acting.

Her supporters argue caution is the only thing preventing panic.
