# Aurelune
("OR-uh-loon")

## Overview

Aurelune is associated with civilization, commerce, law, and prosperity.

Worship of Aurelune is strongest in major cities and trade centers, where merchants and magistrates alike invoke the god's favor.

## Divine Teachings

### Edicts
- bring civilization to the frontiers
- earn wealth through hard work and trade
- follow the rule of law

### Anathema
- engage in banditry or piracy
- steal
- undermine a law-abiding court

## Areas of Concern

- cities
- law
- merchants
- wealth

## Religious Symbol

A golden key.

## Sacred Animal

Monkey.

## Sacred Colors

- gold
- silver

## Worship

Temples of Aurelune often function as:
- courts
- banks
- archives
- centers of civic authority

Priests are expected to maintain order and ensure fair trade practices.
