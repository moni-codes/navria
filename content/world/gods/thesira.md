# Thesira
("Theh-SEER-ah")

## Overview

Thesira represents disciplined warfare, restraint, sacrifice, and the belief that peace must sometimes be protected through strength.

Her followers are often soldiers, guardians, duelists, and martial ascetics.

## Divine Teachings

### Edicts
- protect those who cannot defend themselves
- master your emotions before your weapon
- seek peace before violence
- train body and mind with discipline
- bear suffering so others do not have to

### Anathema
- revel in cruelty or slaughter
- draw steel for pride alone
- escalate conflict unnecessarily
- abandon comrades in battle
- mistake mercy for weakness

## Areas of Concern

- disciplined warfare
- restraint
- sacrifice
- martial devotion
- peace through strength

## Religious Symbol

A broken sword bound in white cloth.

## Sacred Animal

Vulture.

## Sacred Colors

- crimson
- white

## Worship

Temples of Thesira often double as:
- training halls
- shelters
- military sanctuaries
- monasteries

Her followers believe true strength is measured by restraint.
