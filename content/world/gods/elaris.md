# Elaris

## Overview

Elaris is the keeper of stories, memory, and history.

Followers of Elaris believe that truth survives through remembrance, and that forgotten history is among the greatest dangers to civilization.

## Divine Teachings

### Edicts
- preserve stories and histories
- listen before passing judgment
- record truth even when uncomfortable
- ensure the forgotten are remembered
- share wisdom through tales and song

### Anathema
- destroy historical records out of spite or convenience
- silence another’s story for personal gain
- spread falsehoods that erase truth
- allow important knowledge to vanish through neglect

## Areas of Concern

- stories
- memory
- history
- oral tradition
- legacy

## Religious Symbol

An open eye above an unfurling scroll.

## Sacred Animal

Moth.

## Sacred Colors

- deep blue
- parchment white
- copper

## Worship

Followers of Elaris are commonly:
- historians
- archivists
- storytellers
- wandering chroniclers
- musicians
- scholars

Many temples function more as libraries and archives than traditional places of worship.

## Witnesses of Elaris

A Witness of Elaris is a script or record said to have been left behind by Elaris personally.

These writings recount major events from distant ages, including eras before mortal civilization existed.

Those who read a Witness are often changed by the experience, gaining strange insight or power connected to the event described within.

Witnesses remain among the most valuable and dangerous artifacts in Navria.
