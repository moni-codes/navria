# Madrigal
("ma-druh-gull")

## Overview

Madrigal is the patron deity of pirates, sea raiders, monsters of the deep, and those who seize freedom through strength and defiance.

Sailors both fear and revere Madrigal.

## Divine Teachings

### Edicts
- sail the seas
- stay loyal to captain and crew
- take what you want

### Anathema
- betray shipmates
- forsake piracy
- settle permanently on land

## Areas of Concern

- piracy
- sea monsters
- strife

## Religious Symbol

A skull and crossbones.

## Sacred Animal

Parrot

## Sacred Colors

- black
- white

## Worship

Shrines to Madrigal are commonly hidden aboard ships, within smuggler dens, or near dangerous coastlines.

Followers believe the sea rewards boldness and punishes hesitation.
