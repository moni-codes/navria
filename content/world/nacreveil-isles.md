# Nacreveil Isles
The Nacreveil Isles are a chain of tropical islands along the Sirocco Coastline.

## Kaivara (Main Island)
The political and population center.

Known for:
- noble estates
- massive reef harbors
- spice markets
- jungle interior
- Tidewarden headquarters
- strange ancient ruins inland

Closest island to where the castle emerged.

### Iridesca (Main city on Kaivara)
Capital city on southwest coast.
Lysoria’s seat of power.

## Molokai (Industry & Coral Harvesting)
Known for:
- reef trimming
- coral shaping
- shipwrights
- underwater scaffold mining
- dangerous storms

This is where the Liege House "Bluewater-Pierce" has most influence.

Workers here are increasingly pro-mainland influence because the Liege House brings:
- money
- kordite tech
- trade promises

## Halea (Pearls & Spirituality)
Known for:
- pearl diving
- sea temples
- funerary traditions
- whale migrations

A quieter island.
People here distrust mainlanders intensely.
Tidewardens recruit heavily from Halea to help mend the gap of distrust.

## Namaka (Jungle & Anadi Territory)
Dense interior jungle.

Known for:
- medicinal plants
- venom harvesting
- giant insects
- silk weaving
- Anadi settlements

Most outsiders only know the coastline.
Interior is semi-autonomous and difficult to navigate.

## Olohe (Remote & Weirdness)
Farthest island.

Known for:
- deep-sea fishing
- strange tides
- ghost stories
- ancient sea caves

People vanish here sometimes.
