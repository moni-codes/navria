# Kaivara

The political and population center of the Nacreveil Isles.

Closest island to where the drowned castle emerged.

## Known For

- noble estates
- massive reef harbors
- spice markets
- jungle interior
- Tidewarden headquarters
- strange ancient ruins inland

Kaivara serves as the administrative heart of the Isles, where trade, politics, and naval power converge beneath the authority of the Tide Regent.

The island’s coastal cities are prosperous and densely populated, while the inland jungles remain humid, dangerous, and only partially explored.

## Iridesca

Capital city on the southwest coast.

Seat of [[world/figures/lysoria-nacreveil|Lysoria Nacreveil]].

Known for layered harbor districts, pearl-lit canals, and sprawling cliffside estates overlooking the sea.

## Current Tensions

Since the emergence of the drowned castle, Kaivara has become the center of mounting political anxiety throughout the Isles.

Rumors spread daily concerning:
- vanished expeditions
- strange blood-coated pearls
- merfolk sightings
- inland ruins awakening
- disappearances along the coast
